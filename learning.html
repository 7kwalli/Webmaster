Welcome to Cosmic Cravings, where flavor meets the stars! Founded by Giana Smith in the heart of Alma, Arkansas, our mission is to serve fresh, locally sourced ingredients while creating a dining experience that feels truly out of this world. We take pride in partnering with local farmers to bring you the highest quality dishes, all crafted with love and a touch of cosmic inspiration. Whether you're stopping by for a morning coffee, a hearty lunch, or a special dinner, we promise an atmosphere that's just as inviting as our food.



At Cosmic Cravings, we believe that food is more than just nourishment—it’s a way to connect, celebrate, and explore new flavors. Our menu is a carefully curated mix of comfort food and adventurous bites, ensuring there’s something for everyone. From our signature celestial lattes to our handcrafted gourmet meals, every dish is made with passion and creativity. So whether you’re dining in, grabbing takeout, or reserving a table for a special occasion, we’re here to make every moment deliciously memorable.
